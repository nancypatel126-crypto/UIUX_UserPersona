About Priya

Priya is a 21-year-old college student from Ahmedabad who regularly uses digital platforms for education, communication and daily activities.

She prefers simple, fast and mobile-friendly applications that allow her to complete tasks without unnecessary steps.

Behaviour

  • Uses smartphone frequently
  • Prefers simple interfaces
  • Looks for quick information
  • Values easy navigation
02 • GOALS

What does Priya want?

Understanding user goals helps designers create useful and user-centred products.

Easy to Use

Wants interfaces that are simple and easy to understand.

Save Time

Wants to complete tasks quickly without unnecessary steps.

Learn Efficiently

Wants clear information that helps her learn and improve.

Mobile Friendly

Expects a smooth experience on smartphones and tablets.

03 • PAIN POINTS

What frustrates Priya?

Identifying pain points helps designers remove barriers from the user experience.

Complicated Interfaces

Too many options and complicated navigation can create confusion.

Slow Experience

Slow loading and delayed responses can reduce productivity.

Poor Mobile Design

Websites that are not responsive are difficult to use on mobile.

Unclear Information

Confusing content makes it harder to understand what to do next.

04 • MOTIVATIONS

What motivates Priya?

01

Learning

Improving knowledge and skills.

02

Productivity

Completing tasks efficiently.

03

Convenience

Getting things done easily.

04

Connection

Staying connected with people and useful information.

05 • DESIGN INSIGHTS

Design for Priya

These insights translate the persona into practical UX design considerations.

Simple Navigation

Keep navigation clear and easy to understand.

Visual Clarity

Use clear hierarchy and readable content.

Responsive Design

Make the experience comfortable across different screen sizes.

Fast Interaction

Reduce waiting time and unnecessary interactions.

PERSONA SUMMARY

Priya needs simple, fast & clear experiences.

A successful design should help Priya complete her tasks easily, understand information quickly and use the product comfortably on mobile devices.
